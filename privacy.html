Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Humidor Journal is a personal cigar journal. This page explains what the app stores, where, and who can see it.

Using the app without an account

Everything you enter stays on your device. It is stored in your browser's storage and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app's data, or using "Delete all my data," removes it.

If you create an account

Your account stores:

  • Your email address and password (the password is stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Your journal entries, humidor, profile details, vault reviews and achievements, so they're backed up and can sync between your devices.
  • Your confirmation that you are 21 or older, and the date you gave it.
  • Who you follow and who follows you.

This data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

Who can see your data

  • People who follow you (by entering your friend code) can see your profile, badges, humidor, and the journal entries you haven't marked "Keep private."
  • Never shared: your email address, your password, your vault reviews, and entries marked "Keep private."
  • You can remove a follower or block someone at any time.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else.

Age

Humidor Journal is for adults 21 and older. By creating an account, you confirm you are 21 or older.

Deleting your data

You can delete your journal at any time from the Profile tab. When account deletion is available in the app, deleting your account removes everything stored with it. Until then, contact us and we'll delete it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.
